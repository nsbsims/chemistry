Notes

How an ICE table works
  • I, Initial: the concentrations before any reaction, in mol L⁻¹. If you are given moles, divide by the volume first.
  • C, Change: reactants decrease and products increase, in the ratio of the coefficients in the balanced equation.
  • E, Equilibrium: E = I + C. Only these values go into the equilibrium expression.

Perfect squares: when the top and bottom of the expression are both squared, take the square root of both sides to get a simple equation in x.

Small x: when K is very small, hardly any reactant is used up, so (initial − x) ≈ initial. Check afterwards that x is less than 5% of the initial concentration. The quadratic formula is not needed.

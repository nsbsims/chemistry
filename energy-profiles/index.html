What this model assumes

Each rate depends on the fraction of collisions with at least the activation energy, taken as e−Eₐ/RT. The reverse activation energy is always Eₐ(forward) − ΔH, because both directions pass through the same peak.

To keep the numbers readable, K is set to exactly 1 at 400 K for every reaction you build. Below 400 K an exothermic reaction favours products, and above 400 K it favours reactants. An endothermic reaction does the opposite.

Real activation energies are usually far larger than the average collision energy, which is why the bars are drawn on a logarithmic scale.

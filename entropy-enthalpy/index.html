Module 5 · Static and Dynamic Equilibrium

Why Some Reactions Never Come Back

Enthalpy and entropy each push a reaction forwards or backwards. When both push the same way the reaction runs to completion or not at all. When they compete, you get an equilibrium.

Enthalpy term + entropy term = ΔG

Why Some Reactions Never Come Back

Enthalpy and entropy each push a reaction forwards or backwards. See what happens when they agree, and when they compete.

How to use
  1. Choose a reaction.
  2. Slide the temperature and watch whether ΔG changes sign.
  3. Decide which factor wins: enthalpy or entropy.

An observable equilibrium needs ΔG close to zero, so that neither direction is overwhelmingly favoured. This model treats ΔH and ΔS as constant over the temperature range.

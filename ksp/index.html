Notes

Q, Ksp and saturated solutions

For a saturated solution in contact with its solid, such as PbI₂(s) ⇌ Pb²⁺(aq) + 2 I⁻(aq), the equilibrium expression is Ksp = [Pb²⁺][I⁻]². The solid is left out.

  • Q < Ksp: the solution is unsaturated. No precipitate forms.
  • Q = Ksp: the solution is exactly saturated.
  • Q > Ksp: too many ions are in solution. A precipitate forms until Q falls to Ksp.

When two solutions are mixed each one dilutes the other, so always find the new concentrations using the total volume before calculating Q.

The Ksp values here are for 25 °C. In an exam, use the values on your data sheet.
